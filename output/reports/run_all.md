# run_all: the six tasks in sequence

**1. figures_and_output (PR #30, merged).** Every figure in `src`, `examples` and `scripts` is now written by `acoustic_sim.plotting.save_figure`, which refuses a figure with more than one panel (two sharing an axis with a flag), narrower than 1600 px, with text below 11 pt, a missing axis or colour bar label, or a title that does not name the recording; `tests/test_figures.py` scans the code for grids and stray `savefig` calls and tests every refusal. Every grid was split into one figure per panel and every figure the docs reference was regenerated (recording figures from the cached gathers with a new `--figures-only`, the propagation error from its JSON with a new `--plot-only`, the walkthrough, the chain on all 17 recordings): committed PNGs 270 before, 254 after. `output/` now has the layout of `AGENTS.md` (`recordings`, `reproduce`, `walkthrough`, `studies`, `air`, `reports`), checked by `scripts/clean_outputs.py --check`; the history folders (`harbor_demo`, `harbor_domain`, `harbor_cancel`, `harbor_picture`, `performance`, `harbor_detect`, `harbor_stream` and others) were deleted and are named at commit `fd12458` in `docs/harbor_history.md`; the stage 0a cancellation gathers were moved to `studies/history/` for `direct_path` (correction, written after task 2: they predated the trace timing correction, could never be read again, and were deleted in task 2). `scripts/reproduce.py` fills `<!-- reproduce:KEY -->` markers and table blocks in `docs/harbor.md` from `summary.json` and `--check` fails on a stale one; the status paragraph and the "What they say" bullets were rewritten against the k space numbers, and the sentences the numbers no longer supported are listed in the PR. Criteria: no number moved (`--check` after the chain rerun: 1676 numbers, 0 FAIL, compute and latency informational, `summary.json` not updated); `pytest tests` passed (265 passed, 6 skipped in the container, the 10 torch tests on the host). What did not work: the walkthrough of `bistatic` ran out of GPU memory in the first rerun (the desktop holds 2 GB of the 8 GB card) and passed on a second run; the `02_forward_model` scripts and several history scripts were converted but not run (they need FDTD or history datasets); one corrected prose number: the leapfrog stage 1 bistatic run gave 3 % against 27 %, not the 2 % against 30 % the page said.

**2. direct_path (PR #31, merged).** Six reference estimators of the ECA (`BistaticConfig.ref_mode`: the beam, a maximum ratio weighting of every channel and frequency by its modelled response over its noise, the same on the cable or the hydrophones alone, an iterated estimate that subtracts the echo tracks' modelled echoes, and a joint least squares solve of the waveform and a scale per channel) were scored on the stage 0a cases. The prompt said the cached stage 0a gathers existed; they predated the trace timing correction and could not be read, so the six 10 s FDTD runs were made again with the k space scheme (1001 s on the GPU). Section 1 criterion **pass**: the beam, `weighted`, `iterated` and `joint` reach 20 dB stationary at 193 and 99 m and 15 dB moving at 193 m (on the k space gathers the beam alone already does); `joint` has the best mean over the six cases (22.7 dB, beam 18.3, weighted 21.1) and became the default of every processing config. The chain was rerun and `--update`d; all four chain criteria **fail**: uuv1 on the multistatic seeds 0.00, 0.00, 0.08 against 0.5; none of the seven static bodies tracked on `multistatic_b` against three; five Pd falls of more than 3 points (simple seed 1 uuv1 23 to 0 %, simple seed 2 wreck 86 to 66 %, multistatic seed 2 uuv1 30 to 0 % and uuv2 89 to 72 %, multistatic seed 3 uuv2 86 to 81 %); compute 1.13 to 2.00 s per second of data against 1.5 (0.97 to 1.58 before). Moved the other way: the `multistatic_b` UUVs 27 to 40 % and 45 to 68 %; false tracks on the bistatic recordings rose from 6 to 9 to 9 to 12 per minute. The measured direct path removal on the recordings (truth components through the chain's projections, on four recordings only) is 6 to 15 dB on the multistatic recordings for both the beam and `joint`, against 22.6 to 26.5 dB for a lone ship: on the recordings the reference is not the limit, and the other ships' direct sound in the traces is the likely one (an inference, not a separate experiment). What did not work: `iterated` cannot differ from the beam on the stage 0a cases (no body); the size criterion of stage 2 is still untestable (no piling or hull tracked); the bistatic walkthrough ran out of GPU memory again in the full rerun and passed on a second run.
