# run_all: the six tasks in sequence

**1. figures_and_output (PR #30, merged).** Every figure in `src`, `examples` and `scripts` is now written by `acoustic_sim.plotting.save_figure`, which refuses a figure with more than one panel (two sharing an axis with a flag), narrower than 1600 px, with text below 11 pt, a missing axis or colour bar label, or a title that does not name the recording; `tests/test_figures.py` scans the code for grids and stray `savefig` calls and tests every refusal. Every grid was split into one figure per panel and every figure the docs reference was regenerated (recording figures from the cached gathers with a new `--figures-only`, the propagation error from its JSON with a new `--plot-only`, the walkthrough, the chain on all 17 recordings): committed PNGs 270 before, 254 after. `output/` now has the layout of `AGENTS.md` (`recordings`, `reproduce`, `walkthrough`, `studies`, `air`, `reports`), checked by `scripts/clean_outputs.py --check`; the history folders (`harbor_demo`, `harbor_domain`, `harbor_cancel`, `harbor_picture`, `performance`, `harbor_detect`, `harbor_stream` and others) were deleted and are named at commit `fd12458` in `docs/harbor_history.md`; the stage 0a cancellation gathers were moved to `studies/history/` for `direct_path` (correction, written after task 2: they predated the trace timing correction, could never be read again, and were deleted in task 2). `scripts/reproduce.py` fills `<!-- reproduce:KEY -->` markers and table blocks in `docs/harbor.md` from `summary.json` and `--check` fails on a stale one; the status paragraph and the "What they say" bullets were rewritten against the k space numbers, and the sentences the numbers no longer supported are listed in the PR. Criteria: no number moved (`--check` after the chain rerun: 1676 numbers, 0 FAIL, compute and latency informational, `summary.json` not updated); `pytest tests` passed (265 passed, 6 skipped in the container, the 10 torch tests on the host). What did not work: the walkthrough of `bistatic` ran out of GPU memory in the first rerun (the desktop holds 2 GB of the 8 GB card) and passed on a second run; the `02_forward_model` scripts and several history scripts were converted but not run (they need FDTD or history datasets); one corrected prose number: the leapfrog stage 1 bistatic run gave 3 % against 27 %, not the 2 % against 30 % the page said.

**2. direct_path (PR #31, merged).** Six reference estimators of the ECA (`BistaticConfig.ref_mode`: the beam, a maximum ratio weighting of every channel and frequency by its modelled response over its noise, the same on the cable or the hydrophones alone, an iterated estimate that subtracts the echo tracks' modelled echoes, and a joint least squares solve of the waveform and a scale per channel) were scored on the stage 0a cases. The prompt said the cached stage 0a gathers existed; they predated the trace timing correction and could not be read, so the six 10 s FDTD runs were made again with the k space scheme (1001 s on the GPU). Section 1 criterion **pass**: the beam, `weighted`, `iterated` and `joint` reach 20 dB stationary at 193 and 99 m and 15 dB moving at 193 m (on the k space gathers the beam alone already does); `joint` has the best mean over the six cases (22.7 dB, beam 18.3, weighted 21.1) and became the default of every processing config. The chain was rerun and `--update`d; all four chain criteria **fail**: uuv1 on the multistatic seeds 0.00, 0.00, 0.08 against 0.5; none of the seven static bodies tracked on `multistatic_b` against three; five Pd falls of more than 3 points (simple seed 1 uuv1 23 to 0 %, simple seed 2 wreck 86 to 66 %, multistatic seed 2 uuv1 30 to 0 % and uuv2 89 to 72 %, multistatic seed 3 uuv2 86 to 81 %); compute 1.13 to 2.00 s per second of data against 1.5 (0.97 to 1.58 before). Moved the other way: the `multistatic_b` UUVs 27 to 40 % and 45 to 68 %; false tracks on the bistatic recordings rose from 6 to 9 to 9 to 12 per minute. The measured direct path removal on the recordings (truth components through the chain's projections, on four recordings only) is 6 to 15 dB on the multistatic recordings for both the beam and `joint`, against 22.6 to 26.5 dB for a lone ship: on the recordings the reference is not the limit, and the other ships' direct sound in the traces is the likely one (an inference, not a separate experiment). What did not work: `iterated` cannot differ from the beam on the stage 0a cases (no body); the size criterion of stage 2 is still untestable (no piling or hull tracked); the bistatic walkthrough ran out of GPU memory again in the full rerun and passed on a second run.

**3. tracker_uncertainty (PR #32, merged).** Every track is now a constant velocity Kalman filter (Mahalanobis gate at the 0.99 quantile, measurement noise from the map peak's half maximum widths scaled by `meas_scale`, process noise 0.05 m/s² for radiator and 0.2 m/s² for echo tracks); sidelobes are decided by the array's response between a block's peaks and the confirmed tracks instead of a 30 m disc; a radiator track about to confirm is tested for being an illuminator's echo (coherence at least 0.7 and on an echo map peak); static bodies are scored by the distance to their outline; `picture.jsonl` carries the covariances (`harbor_picture/2`). `meas_scale` (a new parameter) was set on the tuning recordings only: 0.25 for the radiator tracks, 0.1 on the 5 m harbor, 1.5 for the echo tracks. Criteria: false tracks halved on every test recording and at most 2 on `multistatic_b` **fail** (halved on simple seeds 1 and 2 and bistatic seed 1 only; multistatic seeds 6, 6, 9 and `multistatic_b` 7.0 per minute unchanged); the simple wreck at least 0.9 on every test seed **pass** (1.00 on all three, from 0.67, 0.66, 0.97); no Pd fall above 3 points **fail** (`multistatic_b` boat 80 to 70 %, bistatic seed 1 UUV 73 to 70 %); the 95 % ellipse holding the truth in 85 to 99 % of fixes **pass** (94.3 % of 2926; 79 to 100 % per recording, below 85 % on the 5 m harbor, above 99 % on the multistatic seeds). Moved: the multistatic uuv1 0 to 8 % to 62 to 84 %, uuv2 72 to 81 % to 94 to 95 %, the moored hull 0 % to 77 to 81 % (partly the outline scoring), the simple harbor's UUV 0 to 28 % to 31 to 66 %, the `multistatic_b` tug 86 to 100 %; compute on the 0.5 m recordings 1.13 to 2.00 to 1.44 to 2.31 s per second of data. The wreck's continuity was measured: the ferry track's predicted map explains 33 of the 34 passive peaks at the wreck as sidelobes, so no radiator track of the ferry's echo forms there and the echo chain no longer drops the wreck's echo peaks. What did not work: the echo test held no track (coherence at most 0.38 against 0.7), so it removed none of the false radiator tracks and the bistatic UUV is still "seen both ways"; the pilings and the diver are still not tracked and the size criterion of stage 2 is still not testable; the half maximum widths as they are made the filter lag on the 5 m harbor (ferry 20 %), hence the scale; a forward model import in the outline scoring broke the contract test and was replaced by the mask computed from the truth's scenario, after which every recording was rescored from its `picture.jsonl` (median errors of the wreck moved within tolerance, nothing else).

**4. ci_and_noise_model (PR #33, merged).** Part 1: `tests.yml` now runs `pytest tests` in full on the CPU on Python 3.10 and 3.13 (290 tests, 253 passed, 37 skipped: the GPU and PyTorch tests, 0 failures, about 3 to 6 minutes, so not split) and prints the skipped count; `gpu-tests.yml` runs the 31 tests now marked `gpu` on a self hosted runner labelled `gpu`, by hand and weekly, with a 10 minute job timeout (which only counts once a runner takes the job: with no runner registered the queued job fails after 24 hours, stated in `docs/ci.md` with the registration steps). No test failed on the CPU for another reason. Part 2: `noise.model = "harbor_v2"` adds snapping shrimp, surface agitation, quay machinery (one FDTD run on the simple harbor, cached), fibre strain noise and interrogator noise with fading, each switched on its own and each number marked in `docs/noise.md`. Measured (read): the shrimp click level, the fading fraction, the traffic band, speed and coherent distance; quoted only: the shrimp latitude range, the wind slope and the sea state levels; assumed: the shrimp rate per metre and band energy, the machinery, the drift, the interrogator floor and shape (no manufacturer publishes a spectrum that was found). The old model stays the default. No criterion (a measurement): with harbor_v2 on simple, bistatic, multistatic and multistatic_b, seeds 0 to 2, the ships hardly move (only the multistatic_b boat, 70 to 57 %), the silent bodies move by tens of points both ways (multistatic uuv1 62 to 83 % to 0 to 6 %, moored hull 80 to 83 % to 34 to 72 %, bistatic UUV 59 to 70 % to 53 to 80 %), false tracks move by up to 9 per minute both ways, compute falls by up to 0.3 s per second of data; on the simple harbor only the quay machinery changes anything (the pump is tracked and counted false). What did not work: the first literature search was interrupted without a result and was redone; a first noise study run failed on the strain cable recordings (the acoustic components were not collapsed from the sample points to the channels), fixed and tested; with the shrimp's band energy assumed 20 dB higher they dominated the band and lost both silent bodies, so the result rests on assumed levels; which component moves the strain cable recordings was not measured one at a time.

**5. sensor_layout_study (PR #34, merged).** `water/coverage.py` maps, from the chart and the layout alone, the strain coverage (fraction of cable channels with a cos² response above 0.5, and weighted by spreading), the aperture of the sensors that see a cell, the shadowing and the predicted resolution; its tests (a straight cable's axis and broadside, a wall shadowing half the sensors, a short array's resolution against 0.886 λ R / L) pass. Nine layouts of the simple harbor were built with the k space scheme and float64 (36 FDTD runs) and run with `simple.json` unchanged on seeds 0 to 2; no variant failed on a loop specific setting. No criterion (a measurement): the straight leg, the L and the zigzag never track the boat; the U and the loop track every ship; in strain mode no layout tracks the UUV and the loop tracks the wreck in 24 to 31 % (mean) against 100 % on the pressure loop of `main`, while the zigzag tracks it in 90 %; hydrophones bought nothing; over every variant, seed and object the strain coverage at the object correlates best with Pd (Spearman +0.54), the aperture best for radiating objects (+0.59), nothing predicts the silent bodies (at most +0.28), and shadowing is constant in this open basin. What did not work: the prompt's equal cable length within 10 percent cannot hold in a 500 m basin (the straight leg is 400 m, the L 710 m, the zigzag 566 m against the loop's 1176 m), so for those variants length and shape are mixed; **the GPU failed during the study** (the driver reports the Quadro RTX 4000 in an error state and CUDA sees no device), so variant P was run on the CPU and its compute is not comparable; the GPU tests were skipped in the final test run.

**6. float32 (PR #35, open, not merged: the scattered component criterion failed).** The GPU came back after a reboot and the GPU half was done on the Quadro RTX 4000. Section 1, the direct traces: **pass** on the GPU as on the CPU (the direct arrival moves by 0.0000 ms at every hydrophone, the traces agree within 4.6e-4 at the worst receiver and 5.7e-5 at the median). Time per step float64 / float32 on the Quadro: 1.42 / 0.32 ms (simple), 2.46 / 0.60 (bistatic and multistatic_b), 0.65 / 0.30 (harbor); memory per run halved (108 to 54 MB on the 601 x 801 grid); the float32 build took 51 to 898 s of FDTD per recording against 356 to 6079 s. Found on the way: the float32 k space runs on the 601 x 801 grids differed between processes (about one fresh process in three, one in ten by a disturbance up to 2e-2 of the peak); float64 never; the cause was not found; the fused k space step now waits for the device after every operation in float32 (float64 never affected, no waits), which brings the large differences to 1 run in 240 and costs about 40 % of the float32 step (`output/studies/float32/gpu_determinism.md`). The scattered component criterion (within 1e-3 of its rms): **fail**. On simple, bistatic, multistatic and the ferry and tug of multistatic_b every scattered component is within 3.3e-4, but the boat's two runs on multistatic_b, run together as one batch, diverged (the field grew without bound after 15 to 25 s) in the build, when built again and in every further batched run, with or without the bodies and with launch blocking, while the same run alone did not (to 30 s) and float64 never did; the cause was not found (`batched_divergence.md`). So float64 stays in every scenario file, the recordings were put back to their float64 build, `reproduce.py` was not rerun and no number moved (`--check`: 1270 pass, 0 fail). Section 2 on the GPU: the mixed precision ECA removes the direct path within 2e-7 dB of double on the stage 0a cases and on the recordings, echo peaks identical: **pass**, but it is 3.8 to 4.9 times **slower** on the Quadro too (stage 0a joint reference and ECA 0.36 to 0.48 s per second of data in double, 1.75 to 1.83 mixed; the echo chain on multistatic 1.51 to 5.43), so `eca_precision` stays `float64`. What did not work: the first fix of the run to run differences (a wait before the first step) did not remove them; waits around the FFTs alone did not either; the per operation waits do not remove them fully; the batched float32 divergence was not explained.
