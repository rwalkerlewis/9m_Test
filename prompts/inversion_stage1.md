## Goal of the track

The goal is one inversion over the whole harbor domain that produces a reflectivity image of every scatterer, using ship noise as the sources, with detection done on that image. The planned stages are:

1. Linear (single scattering) sparse inversion with known sources and the scattered field only. **This task.**
2. Joint inversion for the ship source spectra and the reflectivity, with the direct path inside the forward model (the q = 0 term), which replaces ECA and the separate reference.
3. All illuminators in one solve, a static scene over the full record, moving targets on the residual or per short window, and the ship without AIS located passively and added as an unknown source.
4. Nonlinear inversion with the FDTD and adjoint gradients on the GPU, starting from the linear result, on a different grid from the one that generated the data.

Repo convention: every experiment has its pass/fail criterion in the script docstring and `results.json` before it runs; criteria are never changed after a result is seen; parameters (regularization weight, grid spacing, window length) are chosen on development data and reported on held out data.

## Stage 1: linear sparse inversion with oracle sources

### Forward model

Write `src/acoustic_sim/harbor/inversion.py` with a matrix free linear operator. For window k, frequency f, receiver r, illuminator s:

d(r, f, k) = sum over cells x of A(r, x, f) · P(x, s_k, f) · u_s(f, k) · q(x)

where A is the receiver replica of `ReplicaModel` (Green's function at the sample points, collapsed for strain channels, with the optional `DispersionModel`), P is the ship to cell path factor (`ReplicaModel.path`), u_s is the source spectrum (oracle waveform in this stage) and q(x) is a complex reflectivity per cell, constant over the band. Provide `forward(q)`, `adjoint(d)` and the batching needed to fit memory; several illuminators contribute additive terms with the same q. The adjoint applied to the data is the current coherent bistatic map up to normalisation; check that numerically.

Add an option for q to vary over a few sub bands (for example three), coupled by a group sparsity penalty, since the soft body echo is frequency dependent. Report both.

### Solvers

1. Adjoint (the coherent map; the baseline).
2. Damped least squares by conjugate gradients on the normal equations (Tikhonov).
3. FISTA with an L1 penalty on |q| (complex soft threshold), step size from a power iteration estimate of the operator norm.
4. OMP (greedy, the inversion counterpart of CLEAN), stopped by a residual threshold from the noise level.

Choose the regularization weight on development data by the discrepancy principle (residual norm equal to the expected noise norm, estimated from the noise component) and fix it before the held out runs.

### Data

1. **Moving target, one illuminator:** the fine bistatic dataset (`output/harbor_bistatic/fine/dataset.npz`; rebuild with `examples/harbor_bistatic_dataset.py --cuda` if missing). Data = scattered component plus noise, oracle ferry waveform, exact ferry track. Invert per window of 0.5, 1 and 2 s (q per window; the UUV moves 1 to 4 m). Development: noise seed 0. Held out: seeds 1 to 5, mixed from the cached gathers.
2. **Static scene, three illuminators:** the fine multistatic dataset (`output/harbor_multistatic/fine/dataset.npz`; rebuild with `examples/harbor_multistatic_dataset.py --cuda` if missing). Data = scattered components of ferry, tug and boat plus noise, oracle waveforms and exact tracks. Invert one q over the full 20 s record for the static bodies (six pilings 5 m apart, the moored hull), and per 2 s window for the moving bodies (two UUVs, the diver). The moving bodies smear in the full record inversion; report what that does to the static image. The moored hull at y = 262 lies outside the 40 m edge of the stage 0 scan grid: use an inversion grid that ends at the sponge edge (30 m) and state that the hull is close to the sponge.
3. **Analytic check:** synthetic data generated by the same operator from a known sparse q (a few point scatterers) with noise, to validate the solvers. This is deliberately an inverse crime and is used only as a solver test, never reported as performance.

Grid: 2 m for development, then 1 m; report both. Run each case with and without the dispersion model (the dispersion model uses knowledge of the FDTD grid, so the results without it are the honest ones for real data).

### Metrics

1. **Threshold:** from noise only data (scattered component removed, same chain), set T per solver so that a fraction 0.05 of windows have any cell above T.
2. **Pd per body:** the fraction of windows in which the image has a peak above T within 3 m of the body (for static bodies, report detected or not on the full record image, with T from the full record noise only image at the 95th percentile over noise seeds).
3. **Localization error** of the detected peaks.
4. **Peak to sidelobe ratio:** the image peak at the body over the highest image value more than 5 m from any body, in dB.
5. **Resolution:** point spread functions of the operator (the image of a single point scatterer through adjoint and through each solver) at several positions in the domain, for one illuminator and for three, and whether the six pilings 5 m apart are separated.

### Criteria, fixed now

1. Moving target, one illuminator, held out seeds: FISTA reaches at least the Pd of the adjoint map at the 0.05 false alarm rate and improves the median peak to sidelobe ratio by at least 6 dB.
2. Static scene, three illuminators, full record: the inversion detects at least five of the six pilings as separate peaks and the hull, each within 3 m, above T.
3. Three illuminators against the ferry alone on the static scene: the joint inversion detects at least as many static bodies and improves the median peak to sidelobe ratio by at least 3 dB.

### Outputs

Under `output/harbor_inversion/`:

- `operator_check.json`: adjoint test (dot product test to round off), adjoint versus coherent map correlation.
- `analytic/`: recovery of the known sparse q per solver.
- `bistatic/`: images per solver for selected windows, Pd and peak to sidelobe ratio per solver and window length, development against held out.
- `multistatic/`: full record static images per solver, ferry alone against three illuminators, per window moving body images.
- `psf.png`: point spread functions.
- `results.json` and `run.log` in each.

Script: `examples/harbor_inversion_stage1.py` (with flags for the case, solver, grid spacing and window length).
